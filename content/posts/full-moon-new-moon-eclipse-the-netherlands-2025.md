## Eclipses in 2025 — visibility from the Netherlands

There are **4 eclipses in 2025** (2 lunar, 2 solar). Summary and Netherlands-specific visibility below. [EclipseWise](https://www.eclipsewise.com/oh/ec2025.html)[Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/eclipse/2025)

1) **Total lunar eclipse — 13–14 March 2025
⁠
⁠Global event:** Total lunar eclipse (13–14 Mar 2025). [EclipseWise](https://www.eclipsewise.com/oh/ec2025.html)

**Netherlands (Amsterdam) visibility:** *You could see the beginning phases before moonset* — the eclipse was **visible in the Netherlands at moonset** (penumbral and the early partial phases), but the Moon set before the central totality and the greatest phase was below the horizon for Amsterdam.

**Local times (Amsterdam):**

- Penumbral begins: **Mar 14, 2025 — 04:57** (visible).
- Partial begins (U1): **~06:09** (very low on horizon / visible).
- Totality begins: **~07:25** (NOT visible from Amsterdam — Moon already below the horizon for the deeper phases).
- Greatest eclipse (UTC 06:58) corresponds to **07:58 CET** (not fully visible from Amsterdam).

(Detailed local timetable available from TheSkyLive/TimeandDate). [TheSkyLive](https://theskylive.com/lunar-eclipses/2025-03-14?cc=NL)[Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/eclipse/lunar/2025-march-14?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 2) **Partial solar eclipse — 29 March 2025**

### **Global event:** Partial solar eclipse (Mar 29, 2025). [EclipseWise](https://www.eclipsewise.com/oh/ec2025.html)

- **Netherlands visibility:** **YES — visible as a partial solar eclipse** across the Netherlands.
- **Local times (example: Amsterdam):** approximate local timings reported: **start ≈ 11:12 CET**, **maximum ≈ 12:10 CET**, **end ≈ 13:07 CET** (maximum coverage in Amsterdam ~25% of the Sun). Use proper solar filters/eclipse glasses for safe viewing. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/eclipse/in/netherlands?iso=20250329&utm_source=chatgpt.com)[TheSkyLive](https://theskylive.com/solar-eclipse?cc=NL&id=2025-03-29&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 3) **Total lunar eclipse — 7–8 September 2025**

**Global event:** Total lunar eclipse (7–8 Sep 2025) — one of the longest total eclipses of 2025. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/eclipse/2025)

**Netherlands (Amsterdam) visibility:** **YES — the Moon rises while the eclipse is already in progress**, so observers in the Netherlands will **see the ending part of totality and the remainder of the event after moonrise**. In other words, you’ll see the Moon already eclipsed when it rises and then watch totality end and the partial phases finish. [TheSkyLive](https://theskylive.com/lunar-eclipses/2025-09-07?cc=NL)

**Local times (Amsterdam) — key contacts (local):**

- Penumbral begins (P1): **Sep 7, 2025 — 17:28:06** (not visible)
- Partial begins (U1): **18:26:51** (not visible)
- Totality begins (U2): **19:30:36** (not visible)
- **Greatest eclipse:** **20:11:46 CEST** (Moon **rising**; check local horizon)
- **Totality ends (U3): 20:53:18 CEST** — **visible** (Moon altitude becomes positive around this time).
- Partial ends: **21:56:53 CEST** (visible).
- Penumbral ends: **22:55:27 CEST** (visible).
*(Detailed timeline and visibility flags from TheSkyLive/TimeandDate.)* [TheSkyLive](https://theskylive.com/lunar-eclipses/2025-09-07?cc=NL)[Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/moon/phases/netherlands/amsterdam)

### 4) **Partial solar eclipse — 21 September 2025**

- **Global event:** Partial solar eclipse (21 Sep 2025). [EclipseWise](https://www.eclipsewise.com/oh/ec2025.html)
- **Netherlands visibility:** **No — not visible from the Netherlands.** This eclipse’s visibility is mainly over the South Pacific / New Zealand / Antarctica. (Several eclipse trackers mark it as **not visible** from Europe).
