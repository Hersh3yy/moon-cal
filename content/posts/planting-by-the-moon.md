It's the time of the year to make your vegetable garden ready for planting seeds and crops. The **lunar cycle** has been used in traditional farming practices for centuries, influencing when farmers plant, sow, and harvest crops. The idea is that the moon's gravitational pull affects **soil moisture and plant growth**, similar to how it affects tides. Here’s how it works:

### **A Waxing Moon 🌙  (from a New Moon to a Full Moon)**

- **Best for planting above-ground crops** (leafy greens, grains, tomatoes, corn, beans, etc.).
- Increased moonlight is believed to promote leaf and stem growth.
- Rising water levels in the soil help seeds germinate quickly.

### **Full Moon 🌕 **

- **Strongest gravitational pull**, drawing moisture to the surface.
- Ideal for **transplanting, grafting, and fertilizing**.
- Some believe it enhances plant vitality and nutrient absorption.

### **Waning Moon 🌖 (From Full Moon to a New Moon)**

- **Best for planting root crops** (carrots, potatoes, onions, garlic, beets).
- Decreasing moonlight encourages root development rather than leaf growth.
- A good time for **pruning, harvesting, and weed control**, as sap flow is lower.

### **New Moon 🌚 **

- **Rest period for plants**, as soil moisture is at its lowest.
- Best time for **tilling, preparing soil, and removing weeds**.
