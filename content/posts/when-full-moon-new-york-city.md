**Perigee:** The point in the Moon's orbit where it is closest to Earth is called perigee. 

[**Full Moon**](https://www.google.com/search?rlz=1C5CHFA_enNL1022NL1022&cs=0&sca_esv=c6427501e1c4b628&sxsrf=AE3TifNZmxuVJEAINuTrFFcoYHFcCVjxfw:1759147907635&q=Full+Moon&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjf0eXQ-P2PAxXuKvsDHaDLOnYQxccNegQIFBAB&mstk=AUtExfBeYiXNV-L3DSa1cKoTJyNHssqNbdBejNA4KtTrzGo2Fsqtno6et7wQjO6z7pd6PUP4uDfd3gUAoJThQpnpM9RAJJsxCZmFzJ87SWhQ6m1UGwuYaMJ5mrY3Nk_H8V6PLoEbxJZXzuolq-XVPHpyN3uglewFkHcSrchZY2yXKQ4nnxDwt-7TqEv6LWPNJfUMuNKQXn_P11k8Lt2B_dkSiTQvfDnjQbyePL9xkpw8wWupFtzUuIom6EWJSAzvGcBUplqJle9qA_gG4jyQU-F83Vty&csui=3)**:** A full moon occurs when the Sun, Earth, and Moon are aligned, with sunlight reflecting off the entire lunar surface toward Earth. 

**Supermoon Event:** 
⁠
⁠A supermoon happens when a full moon coincides with the Moon's perigee.
