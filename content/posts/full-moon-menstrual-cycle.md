## Are you trying to get pregnant or do you want your natural cycle back? You may have heard that this is linked to the moon. Is that true? And what can you actually do to get your natural cycle flowing. 

While the Moon's impact on menstrual cycles remains unproven, several lifestyle and environmental factors **are proven** to affect hormonal balance and menstrual health. Understanding these factors can empower individuals to make informed choices to support their reproductive well-being.

## Here are 6 tips to get your natural cycle back:

### **1. Nutrition and Diet**

Diet plays a crucial role in hormonal regulation and menstrual health. Consuming a balanced diet rich in whole, unprocessed foods can support hormonal balance and regular menstrual cycles.

**Healthy Fats:** Incorporating sources of healthy fats, such as avocados, nuts, and olive oil, can support hormone production and balance.

**Fiber:** A diet high in fiber, including fruits, vegetables, and whole grains, can help regulate estrogen levels by promoting its excretion.

**Protein:** Adequate protein intake is essential for hormone synthesis and overall health.

Conversely, diets high in processed foods, sugars, and unhealthy fats can disrupt hormonal balance, potentially leading to irregular menstrual cycles and other health issues.

### **2. Exposure to Endocrine Disruptors**

Endocrine disruptors are chemicals that can interfere with hormonal systems, leading to adverse health effects. Common sources include certain cleaning products, personal care items, and plastics.

**Personal Care Products:** Many conventional cleaning products and creams contain chemicals that may act as endocrine disruptors. Opting for fragrance-free and natural alternatives can reduce exposure to these harmful substances.

**Plastics:** Limiting the use of plastic containers, especially for food storage, can decrease exposure to chemicals like bisphenol A (BPA), known for its endocrine-disrupting properties.

### **3. Physical Activity**

Regular physical activity is beneficial for overall health and hormonal balance. Exercise can help regulate menstrual cycles, reduce symptoms of premenstrual syndrome (PMS), and improve mood.
⁠
⁠**Moderate Exercise:** Engaging in moderate-intensity activities, such as walking, swimming, or yoga, can support hormonal health without causing excessive stress on the body.
⁠
⁠**Strength Training:** Incorporating strength training exercises can improve insulin sensitivity and support metabolic health, which is crucial for hormonal balance.

However, it's essential to avoid excessive exercise, as it can lead to energy deficits and disrupt menstrual cycles, a condition known as functional hypothalamic amenorrhea.

### **4. Stress Management**

Chronic stress can negatively impact hormonal balance and menstrual health. Implementing stress-reduction techniques can support regular menstrual cycles.
⁠
⁠**Mindfulness Practices:** Activities such as meditation, deep breathing, and yoga can help manage stress levels.

**Adequate Sleep:** Ensuring sufficient and quality sleep is vital for hormonal regulation and overall well-being.

### **5. Thermal Therapies**

Thermal therapies, such as saunas and ice baths, have gained attention for their potential health benefits, including hormonal balance and improved menstrual health.
⁠
⁠**Saunas:** Regular sauna use may promote detoxification, improve circulation, and support relaxation, potentially benefiting hormonal health.
⁠
⁠**Ice Baths:** Exposure to cold temperatures through ice baths can stimulate the autonomic nervous system and reduce inflammation, which may positively influence hormonal balance.
⁠
⁠While research on thermal therapies is ongoing, incorporating these practices mindfully and in moderation may offer benefits for menstrual health.

### **6. Alternative Therapies**

Alternative therapies, such as acupuncture, have been explored for their potential to improve menstrual wellness.
⁠
⁠**Acupuncture:** This traditional Chinese medicine practice involves inserting fine needles into specific points on the body to promote healing and balance. Some studies suggest that acupuncture may improve hormonal balance, reduce menstrual pain, and regulate cycles.

It's essential to consult with qualified practitioners and consider individual health needs when exploring alternative therapies.

### **Conclusion**

The belief in a connection between the Moon and the menstrual cycle is deeply rooted in cultural and historical contexts. However, scientific research has not substantiated this association, indicating that the similarity in cycle lengths is likely coincidental.

Focusing on modifiable lifestyle factors offers a practical approach to supporting menstrual health. Embracing a nutrient-rich diet, minimizing exposure to endocrine disruptors, engaging in regular physical activity, managing stress effectively, and considering therapies like acupuncture can contribute to hormonal balance and regular menstrual cycles.
