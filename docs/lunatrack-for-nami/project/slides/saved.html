Where things are remembered

Two little memory boxes

The blog box

Lunatrack has a little blog: 30 stories about the Moon. They live in an online cupboard for text, like a shared notes app.

Soon they move into Hiren's own cupboard, called VAMS, so he fully owns them.

Your own box

Your last location is tucked away inside your own browser, like a sticky note on your fridge.

Come back tomorrow and it already knows where you are. Hiren never sees it; it never leaves your device.

Notice what is not saved: no accounts, no passwords, no personal pile of data. A Moon website does not need them.

Programmers call the text cupboard a CMS (Hygraph today, Hiren's own VAMS next) and the fridge sticky note localStorage. Ask Nami: why is it friendlier that your location stays on your own device instead of on Hiren's computer?
