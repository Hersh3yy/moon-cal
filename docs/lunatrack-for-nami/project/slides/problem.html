Why it exists

The Moon changes every night. Where do you look it up?

THE OLD WAY

Go outside and guess. Or hop between weather apps and dusty almanac pages, none of which know where you are standing.

THE LUNATRACK WAY

One page. It knows your spot, draws tonight's Moon, counts the days to the next full moon, and lists them all for the year.

It is made for people who actually love the Moon, not just a date on a calendar.

Before Lunatrack, knowing tonight's Moon meant guessing or digging through sites that do not know your location. Ask Nami: what would YOU want a Moon website to tell you?
