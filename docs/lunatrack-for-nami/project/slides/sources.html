Where the numbers come from

Three helpers, each good at one thing

The storyteller

A space service called a moon API. It knows the pretty words: phase names, "Harvest Moon", viewing tips.

Costs €1 a month

The calculator

Star-math from a book of centuries-old astronomy. Works out any date and every eclipse, right in your browser.

Free · about 110 KB

The photographer

NASA's real Moon photo for this very hour, plus a weather helper that says if clouds will hide it.

Free · coming soon

Why keep the €1 storyteller when the calculator is free? Because only the storyteller knows the names and the friendly advice. The calculator knows the maths but not the poetry.

Each helper is best at one job: words, maths, or pictures. The calculator (programmers call it astronomy-engine) could someday do everything, but the paid storyteller gives the names and tips people love. Ask Nami: if free does the maths, is €1 a month worth paying for nicer words?
