The eclipse card that fixed itself

One day the paid storyteller stopped sending eclipse dates. The card went blank. Instead of giving up, Lunatrack now works eclipses out by itself with its star-math.

12 August 2026, from Amsterdam

2 August 2027, from Amsterdam

The clever part: it also checks if the Moon is above your horizon at that moment. An eclipse you cannot see is not much of a show.

When the data source dropped eclipses, the app computed them itself and even checks whether the Moon is up where you stand. These three were double-checked against the real 2026 and 2027 eclipses.
