How it draws the right shape

It starts with a full, bright Moon, then lays a dark shadow on top. The only question is: how curved is the shadow's edge?

The checkable bit

One full moon to the next takes 29.5 days. So a full moon is 29.5 ÷ 2 = 14.75 days after a new moon.

And the shadow's curve uses the cosine of how far through you are. A quarter in, that is cos of 90° = 0 → no curve → a perfect half Moon.

Just 20 lines of maths, exact every time. No pile of photos to flip between.

The picture is one bright Moon with a shadow laid over it; the shadow edge is an ellipse whose width is the cosine of the phase. Ask Nami: cos of 90 degrees is 0. If the curve is zero, what shape of Moon do you see? (A half.)
